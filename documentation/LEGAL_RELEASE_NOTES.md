### Policy Decisions Locked in v1.0
- Submission creators retain ownership of their submissions.
- Platform receives non-exclusive publication and operational license.
- Published submission data is licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0.
- Organizer-created infographics/research default to CC BY-NC 4.0 unless a page states otherwise.
- Independent convergent strategy creation may receive shared credit.
- Plagiarism/copyright claims require sufficient evidence.
- No intentional analytics telemetry, browser fingerprinting/profiling, or advertising tracking cookies.
- Publicly disclosed participant data is limited to strategy, creator identity, and institute/affiliation by default.
- Contact details are not intended to be publicly published.
- Attribution/anonymization requests may be made by email with valid reasons.
